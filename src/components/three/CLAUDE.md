# Hero 3D layer

An SSR-safe R3F `<Canvas>` (lazy-loaded, CSS ring fallback) rendering three tilted orbits around the portrait, which is the identity circle and acts as a sun: tetrahedrons in `brand-pink` (inner), octahedrons in `brand-teal` (middle), cubes in `brand-yellow` (outer). Shapes use a custom Fresnel shader and may pass in front of or behind the portrait (depth-masked by an invisible sphere). Orbit paths are `white/10` hairlines that fade with depth and light up with a 3s colour trail behind each shape. The portrait casts a soft black shadow with a fine, slowly pulsing yellow glow. A sparse, seeded, twinkling starfield sits behind. Users can drag to rotate (three's `OrbitControls`, mouse/trackpad only, rotate only, eases back to rest); a one-time intro nudge and a corner `drag to orbit` hint aid discovery. All tuning lives in `src/components/three/orbitConfig.ts`. Reduced motion renders a single static frame.
